How we work

Four habits we are not willing to trade.

These are not values on a wall. Each one has cost us something already, which is the only evidence that they are real.

  • We publish the misses. Every evaluation reports where the engine would have been wrong on that operator's history, alongside where it would have been right. We have thrown out our own headline result before, when the scoring behind it turned out to be improper.
  • No number without a source. Every figure on this site resolves to a benchmark, a published paper or a reported operating record, with the run counts behind it.
  • Read-only by design. The engine estimates and forecasts. It touches no control loop, which is why an operator can evaluate us without putting anything at risk.
  • The operator owns the data. Evaluation files are deleted afterwards, and nothing becomes anyone else's training set without an agreement that says so.
